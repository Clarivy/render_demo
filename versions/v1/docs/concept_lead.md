# Concept D — "The Playset" (lead's draft)

28 s, 840 frames @30 fps, 1920×1080. Four colour-blocked tactile vignettes that feel like the paper
teaser's dollhouse rooms come alive, then a finale that assembles them like the teaser grid.

**S1 Lights On — C1 (0:00–0:08, 240 f).** Dark lilac void; a dollhouse corner (blush-pink + butter walls as
rounded slabs, checker rug) holds a candy chair, a chrome mirror ball on a pedestal, a glossy vase, a rubber duck.
0–2 s: a tiny warm glowing orb (point light) springs in and orbits; crisp shadows sweep across walls/floor,
the mirror ball tracks the highlight. 2–4 s: a pink and an aqua orb pop in → overlapping coloured shadows
(additive RGB shadow play). 4–8 s: orbs shrink away, an HDRI blooms in; a tactile "env globe" (glossy
sphere wrapped with the equirect map, on a stand with a chunky dial) spins 360° and the whole lighting
rotates in sync — highlights travel over the chrome, sun shadows wheel around objects, nothing flickers.
Label: "Point lights · Env maps · Any rotation".

**S2 Glass & Caustics — C2 (0:08–0:14, 180 f).** Mint room. Candy-coloured glass hearts/stars/spheres and a
crystal bunny on a cream pedestal; a strong small light sweeps → coloured, rainbow-fringed caustics (kit's
3-IOR fake dispersion) crawl across the floor; a chrome spiral and chrome blobs inter-reflect. Slow orbit.

**S3 Squish & Roll — C3 (0:14–0:20, 180 f).** Tangerine/coral room. A jelly cube bounces with squash & stretch,
chrome balls roll down a candy ramp, rounded dominoes tip in a wave, a blob morphs sphere→torus. At the
peak, a strobe/onion-skin ghost trail freezes the bounce for a beat to show frame-to-frame continuity
(each ghost consistently lit), then motion resumes. Shadows, reflections and caustics move with the objects.

**S4 Every Dot — C4 (0:20–0:24, 120 f).** Lemon room with a framed Seurat (20000-px scan) above a plush
teddy-fabric cushion. A chunky candy-handled magnifying glass glides over the canvas; the camera follows
through the lens in one continuous push from room-wide to extreme macro where individual painted dots
stay crisp. Label: "20K texture".

**S5 LaRenderer — finale (0:24–0:28, 120 f).** Pull back: the four vignettes are rounded tiles in a
honeycomb (like the teaser grid), each playing its clip as an image-sequence texture; tiles pop/settle with
springy overshoot; inflated candy/chrome letters **LaRenderer** drop in with squash & stretch onto the
pastel floor, catching caustics and reflections.

**Glue.** Light-orb motif travels between shots (the S1 orb becomes the S2 caustic light, etc.); cuts on
the 120 BPM bar grid; captions as rounded pill labels.
